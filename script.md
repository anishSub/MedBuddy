# MedBuddy — Demo Video Script

## Format

- Narrator voiceover + on-screen demo actions
- Runtime target: ~2 minutes
- Perspective: patient journey (problem → solution → resolution)

## Script

### Slide 1 — Hook (0:00 – 0:10)

**(Visual: calm hospital/medicine imagery, then a paper chart being searched)**

NARRATOR:
"In a country where healthcare happens in fragments, one patient can end up with paper records, missed doses, and no quick way to reach help."

### Slide 2 — The Patient's Problem (0:10 – 0:30)

**(Visual: patient juggling phone notes, pillbox, crumpled paper)**

NARRATOR:
"Ramesh — a patient managing chronic medication — has his history scattered across paper files, memory, and phone notes. A single emergency, a missed follow-up, and the continuity of care quietly breaks down."

### Slide 3 — Introducing MedBuddy (0:30 – 0:40)

**(Visual: MedBuddy logo / app splash)**

NARRATOR:
"MedBuddy brings the entire care journey into one platform — for the patient, the doctor, and the clinic."

### Slide 4 — The Solution in Action (0:40 – 1:20)

**(Visual: patient dashboard → medical records → medicine tracker → booking → QR pass → emergency)**

NARRATOR:
"With MedBuddy, Ramesh opens his dashboard and sees adherence, upcoming visits, and follow-ups at a glance.
He finds his medical records — prescriptions, lab reports, X-rays — in one place.
He opens the medicine tracker and sees every dose scheduled for the day.
When a consultation is needed, he books it in seconds: picks a call type, chooses a free slot, confirms.
He carries a QR health pass. And when it matters most, one tap on the ambulance, hospital, or emergency number — or the green Call Now button — connects him immediately.

One platform. One patient. One continuous story."

### Slide 5 — Resolution / Impact (1:20 – 1:40)

**(Visual: calm success state, patient smiling/relaxed)**

NARRATOR:
"No more lost records. No more forgotten doses. No more scrambling during an emergency. MedBuddy keeps the patient — and the care team — aligned."

### Slide 6 — Closing (1:40 – 2:00)

**(Visual: MedBuddy logo + tagline)**

NARRATOR:
"MedBuddy. Complete care. One platform."

## Delivery Notes

- Keep narration calm, clear, and patient-centric.
- Visuals should mirror the user flows: dashboard → records → tracker → booking → emergency.
- Pacing: ~130 words per minute.
