# MedBuddy — Demo Video Flow

Maps each section of the demo video script (`script.md`) to the page(s) shown on screen.

## Slide 1 — Title and Hook (Time 0:00–0:30)

- Page: `/` (landing) or `/dashboard` — product intro / dashboard hero.
- Purpose: establish MedBuddy and the one-line promise.

## Slide 2 — Problem (Time 0:40–1:00)

- Pages: `/medicine` and `/records`.
- Purpose: show the patient's fragmented reality — scattered records and missed adherence — before MedBuddy.

## Slide 3 — Solution and Demo (Time 2:00–2:45)

- Page sequence:
  1. `/dashboard` — adherence, upcoming visit, follow-up in one view.
  2. `/records` — prescriptions / lab reports / X-rays with distinct images.
  3. `/medicine` — morning/afternoon/night dose tracker.
  4. `/book-appointment` — doctor, call type, date/time, confirm booking.
  5. `/health-pass` — QR health pass.
  6. `/emergency` — numbers + green Call Now buttons.
  7. `/doctor/scan` — scan health pass → Next → Upload photos → Open Clinical Record.

## Slide 4 — Value

- Pages: `/doctor/dashboard`, `/doctor/patient`, `/doctor/templates`.

## Slide 5 — Built with AI

- Pages: the screens used to demo AI-assisted content generation/prompt flows.

## Slide 6 — Vision and Ask

- Page: closing on `/dashboard` or `/`.
